# Design

Dark is the primary theme. A light theme is included and keeps the same hierarchy.

Neutrals are near-black and off-white, not pure black or pure white. The single interactive accent is blue. Green, amber, and red are status colours and always sit with a word or an icon.

Type: Newsreader for titles and big numbers, Hanken Grotesk for the interface, IBM Plex Mono for codes and times. Three weights or fewer on the sans, two on the display plus italic.

Cards use a 12px radius, a 1px border, and padding instead of heavy shadow. Buttons and inputs use 8px. Status chips are pills. That is the only radius system.

Motion is a short count-up, a live dot, and a chart draw. It stops when the user asks for reduced motion. There is no glass, no gradient text, and no decorative glow.

The login page is a split: the form on the left, the line "Order. Configure. Prove." on the right. The dashboard follows the Efferd Dashboard 5 anatomy (chart, live count, ranked list, sources, vitals) filled with Project One data, in the visual language of the reference screenshots.

The wordmark is the text "ITCraft". No drawn logo.
